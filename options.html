Option A · Two cards, side by side

Drops the stage-label column. Two simple lists: the muted card is what people expect, the dark card is Dr. Tagge. On a phone they stack, traditional first.

Dr. Tagge plans a different recovery from the one you've heard about.

Most people aren't afraid of bunion surgery itself. They're afraid of the recovery.

Traditional surgery often means

  • Recovery dealt with after surgery
  • A larger incision to reach the bone
  • Screws or plates to hold the correction
  • Crutches or a knee scooter to get around
  • Weeks in a protective boot, on crutches

With Dr. Tagge and PRISM

  • Recovery planned before surgery day
  • Small incisions, under local anesthesia
  • No screws or plates
  • Walk out the same day in a sandal
  • Athletic shoes in 2 to 4 weeks

These are typical results. Timeline varies.
