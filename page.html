Questions people ask before they decide

Do you take insurance?

No, the practice doesn't bill insurance. Some patients file for out-of-network benefits themselves, and the practice can provide the paperwork. Coverage isn't guaranteed.

What does it cost?

The cost depends on Dr. Tagge's plan for your foot. If surgery is right for you, you get one all-inclusive quote before you decide. It covers the surgery, the surgical center and your recovery care, including any regenerative treatments. You can pay over time with PatientFi financing.

Is minimally invasive surgery as thorough as open surgery?

Yes, when it's done well. The openings are small, but Dr. Tagge makes the full correction your foot needs. He chooses the least disruptive approach that gets the job done. More surgery isn't automatically better surgery.

Is my bunion too severe, or am I too old?

Not necessarily. Even significant bunions can often be corrected with minimally invasive surgery, and age alone doesn't rule you out. Dr. Tagge will tell you what's possible for your foot.

When can I drive?

Most people can drive once they feel steady in a shoe Dr. Tagge approves, often within 2 to 3 days. Timelines vary.

Will it hurt?

Expect some pain and swelling. Dr. Tagge plans your pain relief as part of your recovery.

Can my bunion come back?

It can, after any bunion surgery. Lowering the chance is part of your plan, and Dr. Tagge will go over your own risk at the consultation.
